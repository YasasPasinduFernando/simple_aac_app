## 1. Study Nature & Regulatory Pathway

### A. Categorization: Prospective Usability & Feasibility Evaluation
This research protocol represents a **prospective observational and usability evaluation** of an assistive digital health software intervention (Augmentative and Alternative Communication mobile application).
- **Not Retrospective**: No prior, unapproved human subject experimentation or child testing has occurred, and none will be reported as approved scientific evidence. The development to date consists of software engineering, iterative interface design, and laboratory testing using synthetic/seed cards.
- **Not a Clinical Trial of a Drug/Biologic**: The intervention is non-invasive software intended as an educational and communication aid. It carries no clinical diagnostic claims, does not alter medical treatment, and does not prescribe therapeutic regimens.
- **Vulnerable Population**: Participants are minors (aged 2–12 years) with neurodevelopmental and communication disabilities (Autism Spectrum Disorder, ADHD, speech/language delays). Consequently, full ethical review with rigorous child safeguarding, parental proxy informed consent, developmental assent monitoring, and strict data privacy is mandatory.
