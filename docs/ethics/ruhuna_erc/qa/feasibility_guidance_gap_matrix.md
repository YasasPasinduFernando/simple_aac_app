# Feasibility guidance and the 8 November 2026 drafts

This note compares the feasibility framework described by the medical student researchers with the documents generated on 8 November 2026. The framework was shared as guidance from a supervisor discussion. It is not a signed protocol, and it is not evidence that any stage has been completed.

The pasted guidance file named in the correction brief was not present in the project folders examined on 8 November 2026. The rows below follow that brief’s account of the guidance: needs assessment, user requirements, content and function, technical feasibility, expert review, usability and acceptability, implementation, cost, ethics, and a pilot. The current research proposal is a 20-page US Letter draft of one supervised sitting. The supervisor review pack is a 10-page internal note. Neither document is the broader feasibility study.

No telephone numbers are recorded here. No interview, consultation, test result or approval is added.

## What must not be inferred

The application source shows that a picture-based application has been built. It does not show that families have used it in research, that experts have validated the words, or that the centre has agreed to host a study. The current proposal remains the narrow draft until Dr Gayani Punchihewa and the two lead student researchers choose otherwise in writing.
