# University of Ruhuna ERC: Submission Verification Checklist

This checklist corresponds directly to the official requirements published on `https://www.medi.ruh.ac.lk/ethics/instructions.html` and verified against the official checklist form.

---

## Part A: Hard Copies Required

| No. | Item Description | Copies Required | Current Status in Package | Required Action Before Physical Submission |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Cover letter written to Chairperson signed by the applicant | 01 | Draft Generated (`forms/01_Cover_Letter_DRAFT.docx`) | Print on official letterhead; wet-ink signature by Principal Investigator. |
| **2** | Completed application form (Annexure UOR/03-001) | 01 | Draft Generated (`forms/02_Human_Research_Application_DRAFT.docx`) | Fill final PI details; print; wet-ink signatures by PI, Supervisors, and Co-Investigators. |
| **3** | Letter of consent from supervisor/s where relevant | 01 | Draft Generated (`forms/03_Supervisor_Letter_DRAFT.docx`) | Print on University of Ruhuna letterhead; wet-ink signature by Academic Supervisor(s). |
| **4** | Detailed research proposal (including study instruments, consent form and information sheet in English, Sinhala & Tamil if relevant, permission seeking letters) | 02 | Draft Generated (`protocol/04_AAC_ERC_Research_Protocol_DRAFT.docx` + `annexures/`) | Print 2 bound/stapled hard copies including all annexures (instruments, consent, information sheets, site letters). |
| **5** | Curriculum vitae of the principal investigator (PI)/s | 01 | Draft Generated (`forms/10_CV_Principal_Investigator_DRAFT.docx`) | Update with latest publications/employment; wet-ink signature by PI. |
| **6** | Certificates of training of PI/Supervisor (on Research Methodology, Basic Research Ethics, Good Clinical Practice Training) | 01 | Draft Dossier Generated (`forms/13_Training_Certificates_PI_Supervisor_DRAFT.docx`) | Attach authentic printed/scanned certificates of GCP & Research Ethics training. |
| **7** | A letter indicating that investigator(s) have undergone training to handle animals (if relevant) | 01 | Formal Declaration Generated (`forms/14_Animal_Handling_Statement_DRAFT.docx`) | Signed statement of non-applicability; no animal handling required. |
| **8** | Receipt of payment of processing fee (according to payment category) | 01 | Attachment Record Generated (`forms/15_Fee_Payment_Receipt_Placeholder_DRAFT.docx`) | Pay fee (LKR 3,000 Category I) at Shroff Counter, Faculty of Medicine, Karapitiya; attach original receipt. |

## Part B: Soft Copies Required (Email to ethics@med.ruh.ac.lk)

| No. | File Name & Scope | Packaging Requirement | Generation Method |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | `01_Complete_ERC_Application.pdf` | **Single PDF file containing all documents from Item 1 to Item 7** in sequential checklist order. Includes full proposal and all annexures. | Compiled locally by `build_erc_package.py` into `.local_erc_private/exports_private/`. |
| **2** | `02_Anonymous_Research_Proposal.pdf` | **Single PDF file containing Item 4 ONLY** (Research proposal, study instruments, consent forms, information sheets, and draft permission letters). **STRICT REQUIREMENT: Names and affiliations of investigators must be removed from all documents in this soft copy.** | Compiled locally by `build_erc_package.py` from anonymized stream into `.local_erc_private/exports_private/`. |

## Submission Logistics

- **Venue**: ERC Office, CIU Building, Faculty of Medicine, University of Ruhuna, Karapitiya, Galle.
- **Office Hours**: 9:00 am – 11:30 am and 1:30 pm – 3:30 pm (Working days).
- **Deadline**: On or before the last working day of the preceding month (before 3:30 pm) to be scheduled for the monthly ERC meeting.
- **Email Contact**: `ethics@med.ruh.ac.lk`
