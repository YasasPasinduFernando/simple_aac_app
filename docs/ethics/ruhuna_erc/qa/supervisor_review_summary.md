# Review summary for Dr Gayani Punchihewa and the student researchers

Version 01. 08 November 2026. Unsigned internal draft. The title is provisional.

## Proposed study

One prospective sitting with each child and caregiver at PRAGATHI Child Intervention Centre, Galle. The sitting asks whether the procedure can be carried out safely and reliably, whether a willing child selects a picture, whether the caregiver can open the categories, and whether the caregiver finds the language and the screen acceptable. “Not attempted” is kept separate from “attempted but unsuccessful”.

There is no control group, no random allocation, and no claim that communication or speech improves.
