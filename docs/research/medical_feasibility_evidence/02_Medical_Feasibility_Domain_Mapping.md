## The Ten Medical Feasibility Domains

The framework below aligns software engineering evidence with healthcare research inquiries. In accordance with medical research standards, technical implementation in code must not be conflated with clinical efficacy or user acceptability.

## Synthesis for Clinical Protocol Drafting

1. **Clear Alignment:** The software provides a solid, verified foundation for picture-based direct selection AAC, multi-card sentence sequencing, offline local storage, and caregiver security.
2. **Mandatory Protocol Boundaries:**
   - The prospective study must be framed strictly as a **usability and feasibility evaluation**, not a clinical efficacy trial.
   - Any references to visual schedules, social stories, or automated emotion recognition must be removed from protocol drafts.
   - The technical limitations of Android system TTS for Sinhala and Tamil must be acknowledged; custom voice recordings should be highlighted as a clinically dependable alternative.
